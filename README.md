# 🌌 Gravity Nexus: Traveler's Odyssey

**Defy gravity. Explore everything. Survive the journey.**

**Gravity Nexus: Traveler's Odyssey** is a vibrant 2D side-scrolling adventure game where an Indian traveler races through an ever-expanding world while being relentlessly chased by a mysterious gravity catcher.

The journey begins in a humble **village** and progressively expands through **towns, districts, states, countries, continents, oceans, underwater worlds, sky realms, heaven, gods' realms, demons' realms, mythical worlds and ultimately space**.

Gravity isn't just part of the environment — **it is the core gameplay mechanic.**

## 🎮 Core Features

* 🧍 Custom Indian traveler sticker character
* 👹 Dynamic enemy/catcher chasing the player
* 🌍 Progressive world-building from Village → Space
* 🌀 Gravity flip and multiple gravity modes
* 🏃 Continuous side-scrolling gameplay
* 🧩 Increasingly complex obstacles
* 🎨 Animated real-world-inspired environments
* 📖 Flipbook-style character and enemy animations
* 💥 Collision detection and physics-based movement
* 🪙 Collectibles, rewards and hidden routes
* 🎁 Perks, lives and replay mechanics
* 🔊 Dynamic environmental audio and sound effects
* 🎙️ Voice feedback for collectible interactions
* 🌈 Bright green-blue-cyan visual identity
* 🌊 Water, air, celestial, mythical and cosmic environments
* ♾️ Endless progression until the player loses

## ⚙️ Gameplay

The player continuously moves forward while avoiding hazards and staying ahead of the chasing catcher.

The player can:

* Run
* Jump
* Flip gravity
* Navigate moving platforms
* Cross rivers and environmental hazards
* Explore hidden paths
* Collect rewards
* Use interactive objects
* Survive increasingly difficult environments

## 🌀 Gravity System

Gravity can dynamically change between:

* Normal gravity
* Inverted gravity
* Low gravity
* Sideways/magnetic gravity
* Environmental gravity zones

Gravity affects player movement, platforms, obstacles and collision behavior.

## 🧩 Obstacle System

Obstacles evolve with the world.

### Physical

Spiked logs, rotating blades, pendulums and collapsing bridges.

### Environmental

Waterfalls, lava, ice, sandstorms, avalanches and strong winds.

### Mechanical

Conveyor belts, crushers, gears, magnetic fields and electrical hazards.

### Adaptive

Tracking hazards, timed traps and enemy-triggered obstacles.

### Gravity-Based

Floating platforms, ceiling spikes, rotating gravity rings and inverted pathways.

### Interactive

Crates, carts, ropes, portals, trampolines and climbable terrain.

## 🎨 Visual Design

The game uses a **bright green → cyan → blue** visual identity.

Each world develops its own visual character while maintaining the overall design language.

Real-image-inspired environments are combined with:

* Parallax scrolling
* Animated water
* Moving clouds
* Environmental particles
* Wildlife
* Lighting effects
* Dynamic foreground elements

## 🔊 Audio

The game features reactive audio throughout the journey:

* Footsteps
* Gravity whoosh
* Enemy warning effects
* Coin collection sounds
* Environmental ambience
* World-specific music
* Victory effects
* Game-over effects
* Cinematic transitions
* Short voice feedback

The game begins with a **5-second cinematic introduction** and uses a bright, energetic final/game-over interface rather than a static dark screen.

## 🚀 Vision

The goal of Gravity Nexus is to transform a simple platforming mechanic into a **continuous visual journey across increasingly larger worlds**.

A player starts by escaping obstacles in a village and eventually masters gravity while travelling through oceans, skies, celestial realms, mythology and space.

**Start small. Defy gravity. Keep running. Reach beyond Earth.**
